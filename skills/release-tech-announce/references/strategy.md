# Tying a release to Prolific strategy

A release lands harder when the reader can see how it moves the company forward. Use this to connect an announcement to a real strategic priority — but only where the link is genuine. A forced strategy tie reads as filler; a real one makes the release feel like it matters.

> **Check currency first.** Strategy shifts each half. The specifics below are Prolific's **H2 2026** priorities as set out by Phelim Bradley. Before leaning on exact figures or priority names, confirm they're still current (ask the user, or check the latest strategy post). The *framing patterns* age more slowly than the specific numbers.

Source: Phelim's H2 2026 strategy post — <https://prolific-ac.slack.com/archives/CC1FZMYR4/p1781705193956329?thread_ts=1780664416.018359&cid=CC1FZMYR4|Slack thread>. Link to it when you cite the strategy, the way the strongest posts link their proof.

## The three H2 2026 priorities

1. **Growth Expansion** — grow AI revenue outside GenMedia ~5x, exiting the year at ~$10m/month in non-GenMedia AI revenue. Diversify away from single-buyer concentration. Get closer to *data* quality and drive successful customer *outcomes*, not just verified participants.
2. **Value Chain Expansion** — commercially validate two new products, with dedicated teams shielded from BAU: **Agentic Environments & Evals** (tracking agent ability; humans giving road markings and corrections; trajectory tracing) and **Benchmarks** (turning what we learn on every project into a benchmark and a proactive sales motion to model builders).
3. **Network Enrichment & Participant Value** — shift from raw participant acquisition to enriching, retaining, and matching the network we have; measured by average revenue per participant. Expand profiling/matching and integrate customer feedback loops (submission + signals endpoints) so reputations update on real-world outcomes.

## The "right to win" framing

Recurring language worth echoing when it fits the release:

- **Behavioral data over knowledge data** — "how models *engage*", not "what models *know*". Human behaviour, taste, and judgment are Prolific's genuine areas of expertise and right to win. Examples: aesthetic preference, personality evaluation, human baselines for evals, uplift and safety testing, persuasion/perception testing, preference distributions across real demographics.
- **Sell ground truth, not labour/time.**
- **Data quality, not only participant quality** — measure, monitor, and report on the quality of the *output* across a whole project; move quality upstream into task design where problems are cheap to fix.
- **Benchmarks as a GTM motion** — capitalise on expertise we already build, sell it more than once.

## How to use it in a post

- Pick the **one** priority the release most credibly advances and name the connection in the "why it matters" section — e.g. "this is a step toward measurable *data* quality and better customer outcomes (Priority 1)".
- If the release has an explicit **OKR/KR**, cite it directly — the strongest posts quote KR text verbatim ("contributes to KR1…", "0% → 10% by end of H2").
- If there's no clean tie, don't manufacture one. Lead with the customer value instead.
